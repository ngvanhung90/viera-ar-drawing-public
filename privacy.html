Viera AR Drawing Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026.

Viera AR Drawing helps children and adults draw on paper using an image guide displayed over the phone camera view. The guide is on the screen; the phone does not project light onto paper.

Your camera, images and drawings

Camera preview and image processing happen on your device. The preview is not recorded. You may choose an image using the system picker, or explicitly capture a reference or finished drawing with the system camera. Original images, guides, drawings and project settings are stored privately on your device. Drawing photos are not uploaded to a Viera server or remote AI service. Use images you have permission to use.

Saving, sharing and deletion

Export and sharing happen when you request them. Android lets you choose the destination or receiving app. Copies outside Viera private storage follow the destination's controls and privacy terms. Local projects, preferences, ad reward progress and cached purchase receipts can be removed through Android clear storage or uninstall. This does not delete original source photos, exported copies or Google purchase records. App backup is disabled; operating-system behavior remains outside the app's control.

Age groups and children

The app asks for a local age range before its drawing functions. It stores an age group, not a name, full date of birth or identity document. The choice can be corrected in Settings. This choice does not itself give advertising consent or verify identity. Before a valid choice, no ads are requested. For the under-13 group, when advertising is enabled, a single ordinary banner may appear on Home or Templates, with child-directed treatment, general-audience content rating and non-personalized ads. Fullscreen rewarded and interstitial ads are not offered to that group. The app does not request the Android advertising ID permission and uses child-directed request settings for this age range.

Google advertising and privacy choices

When advertising is enabled, Free users may see Google AdMob ads. Older eligible Free users may voluntarily watch rewarded ads for local template progress or see interstitial ads only at eligible breaks. Ads do not appear in active tracing or editing. Pro removes ad surfaces. Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform may process device and service information, ad interactions, diagnostics, IP-derived location and privacy choices to deliver, secure and measure ads. This can include approximate location inferred from an IP address, ad views and taps, performance information and service identifiers. The app is configured for non-personalized advertising. These settings restrict advertising behavior; they do not mean that no data is processed.

The app uses Google's privacy-message flow where required and provides advertising privacy options when required. Eligibility to request ads depends on the current privacy state; an age choice is separate from this state. See Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from partner apps.

Optional Pro purchases

Google Play processes optional monthly and yearly auto-renewing Pro subscriptions. The purchase sheet displays the available localized price, billing period and renewal terms. The app receives purchase identifiers, tokens, state and signatures to verify, acknowledge and restore entitlement; it does not receive your payment-card details. Purchase information is stored privately on the device. Subscription access requires current Google Play ownership checks and is not promised indefinitely offline. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play. Clearing local app data does not cancel a subscription or refund a purchase.

Rewarded templates

For eligible users, each completed rewarded ad grants one noncash, nontransferable template progress point. Three completed ads unlock one selected eligible template locally. This does not grant Pro or paid image-input features. Clearing local data removes these local rewards; no cross-device reward account exists.

Other services and security

No Viera login, developer analytics server or cloud drawing account is required. Included Google Billing, Mobile Ads, UMP and supporting libraries can communicate with Google even though drawing photos remain local. Android app-private storage and network service protections reduce ordinary access risks; they are not a guarantee against a compromised device. SDK operational data and Google records follow Google's retention and deletion controls.
